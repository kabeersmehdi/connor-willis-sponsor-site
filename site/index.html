The driver

Who is Connor Willis?

"Appreciate the challenges and work tirelessly, optimizing every opportunity with faith in the process."

Connor is from Naperville, Illinois. He started karting at 12, won his first car race in Spec Miata from pole, and left high school early to move 4,000 miles to the UK, where the single-seater ladder to Formula One actually runs.

In 2025 he finished seventh in the United Formula Ford Championship and made the Grand Final of the Walter Hayes Trophy. For 2026 he stepped up to GB4, the British Formula 4-spec championship, with Hillspeed, a Derbyshire team with more than 50 years of race-winning history.

His rookie GB4 season brought six straight top-10 finishes to open the year, a first win at Silverstone in August, and a podium in the final race of the season at Brands Hatch. He finished ninth in the championship, and Hillspeed finished runner-up in the teams' standings.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is this real?

Yes. Connor races the number 99 Hillspeed car in the GB4 Championship. His results are on the official GB4 site and his own site, and every race is streamed live. The car on this page is a 3D stand-in used to show where logos go; the real placement is agreed on the actual car before anything is printed.

What do I get?

Your logo on the car or the driver kit for the agreed period, a place on this page and on Connor's website, and mentions across his channels when your artwork goes on. Larger packages can add team kit and race-weekend access, agreed case by case.

How does payment work?

Pick a spot and send your details. Connor's team confirms it is still open and sends a short partnership agreement and an invoice. Nothing is charged on this page.

Can I take more than one spot?

Yes. Bundles and multi-year deals are priced lower per spot than the list. Say what you have in mind in the message box.

Why are the prices what they are?

They reflect how much of the car, and of the TV picture, each surface takes up. The rear wing and the sidepods are the most visible on a single-seater, the halo and nose top are in every onboard shot, and the smaller surfaces are priced accordingly. The full list adds up to the season goal.

What if Connor moves up a category?

That is the plan. If he steps up during your term, your spot carries over to the new car at the same placement.

Can a smaller business join?

Yes. The driver kit and the smaller surfaces exist for exactly that. If none of the list fits, write in and say what budget you have.
