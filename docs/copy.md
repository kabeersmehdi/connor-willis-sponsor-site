## Audit result
The live copy is already plain, specific and sourced, and it follows the competitor patterns the strong pages use. Banned words found: none. Placeholders found: none. Lorem ipsum: none. Weak CTAs: none ("Learn more" and "Click here" do not appear).

One consistency fix applied:
- Navbar button "Get a spot" → "Pick a spot", to match the hero. Pattern: one wording per destination.

## Lines reviewed and kept
- Hero: "Your brand, on Connor's race car." Specific, one idea, no hype.
- Lede: names age, nationality, series and what the money does.
- Hero note: "No payment on this page..." removes the biggest fear in one line.
- Steps: three verbs, each says what happens next.
- Budget: honest about not listing amounts yet.
- FAQ "Is this real?" is the best line on the page. Keep it first.

## Suggestions for the client (not applied, policy decisions)
1. Add amounts to the budget categories once the 2027 plan is set. A number per line will do more than any adjective.
2. When the first spot sells, add one sentence under the bar naming the partner and the spot. Proof next to the claim.
3. If a partner volunteers a quote, place it in the Founding partners section. Do not write one for them.
